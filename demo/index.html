Tuglet sample page

Use this page to check that Tuglet works. Nothing here is sent anywhere.

  1. Click the Tuglet icon in Chrome's toolbar (pin it from the puzzle-piece Extensions menu first).
  2. Choose Try a 1-minute demo reminder.
  3. Under This page, choose Show on this page (temporary) or Always allow for this site.
  4. Wait about a minute. Pip, the star, swings at the top of the page.
  5. Pull Pip down a little and let go to snooze. Pull all the way down (about 120 px) to complete. Or use the buttons.

Scroll: the page keeps working normally.
